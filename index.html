How to Update Data

🟦 Ohio

Download bulk CSVs from the Ohio Report Card download page.

reportcard.education.ohio.gov/download

Files needed: Achievement, Progress, Gap Closing, Overall Summary

🟩 Michigan

Navigate to Student Performance > M-STEP on MI School Data portal.

mischooldata.org

Filter by district/school, export CSV for most recent year

🟧 Missouri

Download MAP assessment data from DESE or MCDS portal.

dese.mo.gov/data-system-management/data-download

🟥 Illinois

Download IAR/PSAT/SAT data from ISBE.

isbe.net/Pages/Illinois-State-Report-Card-Data.aspx

🟦 Iowa

Download ISASP results from Iowa DE.

educateiowa.gov/data-reporting/…

🟩 Minnesota

Download MCA results from MDE.

education.mn.gov/MDE/Data/

🟪 Indiana

Download ILEARN data from IDOE.

doe.in.gov/accountability/…
